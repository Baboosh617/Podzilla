Who Are We.

Podzilla started the way most good ideas do: a group of friends who couldn't stop talking about the stuff they love, deciding to hit record. We're not critics or journalists — just people with opinions, and we want to hear yours too.

Our Process.

We pick a topic, grab a mic and see where the conversation goes. Some episodes stay on track; most don't — and that's half the fun.

Our Approach.

Honest and unfiltered. We say what we actually think, disagree with each other a lot, and always leave room for the other side.

Our Goal.

To make the kind of show we'd want to listen to ourselves: easy to put on, fun to argue with, and never boring.

Our Mission.

To entertain you and to hear from you. Send us your topics, takes and questions, and we'll bring them into the conversation.
